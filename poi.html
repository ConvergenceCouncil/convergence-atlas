3D environment research checklist

For production-quality reconstruction, collect surveyed dimensions, ground plans, elevation and terrain data, multiple exterior angles, interior circulation, architectural materials, lighting, historical changes, and heritage/access restrictions. Photographs alone are insufficient for game-ready geometry.

Do not assume a photograph is licensed for commercial game textures or that a depicted feature matches the approved CONVERGENCE time period.
